# Free video production research — 10 October 2026

## Brief and budget

Produce original architectural cinema for Daman's six verified categories: sheer curtains, blinds, plantation shutters, curtains, security doors and fly screens. Target a silent 24–30 second hero with restrained camera movement, tangible cloth/light detail, stable joinery and a considered loop. Landscape first; separately art-direct portrait shots. Use the existing original concept stills, not someone else's installations. All work remains concept imagery, never proof of a Daman project or product performance.

Budget: no new subscription, card entry, paid trial, credit purchase, API purchase or GPU rental. Existing available account credits can be evaluated, but they are not evidence that a provider's public free tier includes the same entitlement. No watermark removal or region/credit-limit circumvention.

## Free-credit production strategy

First test the hardest shot: real low-amplitude shear-cloth movement with stable architecture. Then test filtered blind sunlight. Inspect before allocating the remaining budget; six mediocre clips are not a quality win. Ask for a single output, not hidden x2/x4 batches.

Flow's official 720p Omni four-second rate suggests six clips could total 42 credits if that exact setup is available. This is a budget calculation, not a generation result. Veo Fast would cost 120 credits for six single outputs at the published rate and therefore require multiple daily allocations. Quality at 100 per output exceeds one free day's allowance. Unused daily credits do not roll over. A provider fallback or auto-switch must be inspected for changed costs/settings.

Adobe's existing 1000-credit entitlement may make a consistent six-shot production possible without a new purchase. Do not consume it blindly: confirm actual selected-model cost and inspect plan restrictions. Native Firefly's commercial-safe positioning does not automatically grant every partner model identical terms. Preserve Content Credentials and any mandatory visible marks.

## Shot prompts

Common direction: photoreal contemporary Australian architectural editorial cinema, warm neutral taupe/ivory/bronze, subtle natural light, restrained contrast, no people, no invented labels or logos, no scene cuts inside a shot. Preserve room geometry, hardware and material identity from the supplied first frame. Avoid rubbery cloth, flicker, bent straight lines and glossy synthetic CGI.

1. `daman-hero-original.png`: sheer linen folds breathe in a very gentle breeze; slow 40 mm dolly toward the window; sun grazes the translucent weave. Cloth motion is small and physically believable; furniture and frames remain still.
2. `daman-blinds-original.png`: soft daylight changes gradually on the blind weave; slow lateral camera glide; a faint leaf-shadow moves outside. Blind hardware stays fixed. No invented motor operation or energy/UV demonstration.
3. `daman-shutters-original.png`: slow glide across precisely parallel louvres and warm stripes of afternoon light. Keep hinges, slat spacing and frame topology stable. No mechanically impossible opening.
4. `daman-curtains-original.png`: close architectural drift beside rich opaque taupe folds into the calm bedroom; grazing highlights reveal cloth texture. Subtle fabric settling, not theatrical wind.
5. `daman-security-original.png`: measured approach toward the screened entrance; warm light across fine mesh and dark metal; stable straight frame. Do not depict a forced-entry test or imply a certification/material grade.
6. `daman-flyscreen-original.png`: stable threshold and fine mesh with small natural foliage motion beyond; minimal camera drift. Mesh remains continuous. No insects passing through or invented screen retraction.

Keep any provider-required watermark intact. Add Daman branding as HTML outside the film, not hallucinated lettering inside it.

## Quality and publication gates

- Download original MP4, record provider/model, generation date, prompt, first-frame filename, debit and output dimensions/duration. Preserve required metadata and licence evidence.
- Watch every clip at normal speed and inspect a contact sheet; reject cloth/mesh/frame morphing, exposure pumping, flash frames and inappropriate product behaviour.
- Match grade, exposure and rhythm without concealing watermarks; export silent fast-start H.264/yuv420p. Genuine object motion must exist before labelling a film generated scene motion.
- Stay under Cloudflare Pages' current asset limits; optimise desktop/mobile separately. Retain reduced-motion, save-data, offscreen pause, poster fallback and accessible controls.
- Review actual commercial-use eligibility and generated concept disclosure before publication. Never imply the scenes are Daman client work.
- Decode all output frames; inspect live desktop/mobile playback after the Git deployment. Green build alone is not the visual gate.
