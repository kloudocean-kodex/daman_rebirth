## Construction accuracy

- **Curtain waves:** continuous vertical drapery, guided by the heading and track. Not horizontal blind folds. [DIYblinds' own S-fold guide](https://www.diyblinds.com.au/curtains/s-fold-curtains), retrieved today, describes continuous waves on a specialised track. This informs a schematic, not a claim that Daman stocks this supplier. The supplementary My DIY Blinds page now redirects to a password-protected page and was not used as refreshed technical evidence.
- **Roller blinds:** a flat panel winds onto a tube; a straight lower bar and stable side edges, not Roman pleats. The generated Daman blind reference depicts this kind of panel. [DIYblinds' roller-blind category](https://www.diyblinds.com.au/blinds/roller-blinds) is a construction reference only.
- **Roman blinds:** material gathers into horizontal folds as it rises. Different constructions do not fold identically. [DIYblinds' soft-Roman product page](https://www.diyblinds.com.au/blinds/soft-roman-blinds), retrieved today, distinguishes unbattened sheer scallops from a batten-built tailored stack. Do not transfer that retailer's lining, light-blocking or performance claims to Daman.
- Pinch, wave and Roman terminology is not interchangeable. A schematic is illustrative, not a fabrication drawing or installation instruction. Width, fullness, heading, fabric weight, lining and stack-back require real specification.

It does **not** show a Daman relationship. Use texture and natural grazing light rather than glossy satin, artificial sparkle or exaggerated gold. Woven depth, a restrained bronze accent and earth tones enrich the existing cream/charcoal identity without turning it into a generic black-and-gold luxury template. Selected swatches change a design note, not an inaccurately recoloured product photo. Screen colours are indicative; use physical samples in the actual room.
