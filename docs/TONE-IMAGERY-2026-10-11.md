# Coordinated tone concept imagery — 11 October 2026

## Production and provenance

Five original colour studies created with the built-in image-generation tool in precise-object-edit mode. No external stock provider, subscription upgrade or paid API was used. Source: the existing generated `public/assets/daman-textile-palette.webp`. Oat became the composition reference for the four subsequent edits. Original PNGs are preserved in `media/generated/tones/`; delivery images are WebP in `public/assets/` at 480, 960 and 1536 pixels wide.

The same translucent sheer, stone and bronze details remain across the series. Both opaque curtain panels change tone. The studies retain continuous vertical curtain waves rather than introducing Roman-blind folds. These are AI-generated conceptual studies, not exact recolour simulations, approved Daman stock, fabric SKUs or completed installations. Small generated texture differences are possible. The owner must confirm real fabric/heading availability; physical samples remain the colour authority.

WebP encoding: FFmpeg libwebp, quality 82, compression level 6, proportional scaling only. Originals remain unchanged. No hue filters or whole-scene colour overlays are used at runtime. Only Oat is initially rendered (lazy); other previews are requested on selection, decoded before replacement, with cancellation of stale requests and preservation of the last successful image on error. Reduced-motion users receive an immediate decoded replacement rather than a fade.
